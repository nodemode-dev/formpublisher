# Code of conduct

FormPublisher is the Form family's site publisher, built in the open. This page says how we treat each other while building it. It applies to this repository, its issues, pull requests, discussions and wiki, and to anyone speaking for the project elsewhere.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, with newcomers above all.
- Argue about code, designs and ideas, never about people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Accept correction gracefully, and give it kindly and specifically.
- Credit the work of others, including the projects and examples you build on.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct, on any grounds.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption of any kind.
- Using FormPublisher or its agents to prepare or spread sites meant to harm the people who visit them, and sharing them here.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code by opening an [issue](https://github.com/nodemode-dev/formpublisher/issues), or by reaching the maintainers on GitHub: the [nodemode-dev](https://github.com/nodemode-dev) organisation, [@nodemode-ai](https://github.com/nodemode-ai) and [@gbarnai](https://github.com/gbarnai). A security problem is a different matter: report it as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says.

## What happens next

The maintainers review every report and decide what is fair: a private word, a public note, removal of content, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces. People who report in good faith will not face retaliation.
